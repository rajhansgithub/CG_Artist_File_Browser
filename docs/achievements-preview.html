Theme: lens iris medals

CG and VFX artists are proud of what comes out of the camera and the screening room. So each medal's face is a camera iris, six aperture blades round the badge icon, and each set is a certified showreel framed on a wall. Diamond and Ruby set a real cut stone in the opening. Emulsion is the live soap bubble with the app's mark.
