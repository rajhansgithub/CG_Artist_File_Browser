## Opening a PDF

PDF files show their first page as the thumbnail. Open one with `Enter` or a double-click. The viewer shows the whole document in one column that you can scroll, so you can read a brief or a shot list without a separate reader.

![The PDF viewer with the toolbar on top and the page below](media:pdf-viewer.webp "Pages scroll in one column. The toolbar holds the page number, zoom and rotation.")

## Moving through the pages

- `Page Down` or `Space` goes to the next page, `Page Up` or `Shift` + `Space` to the previous one.
- `Home` and `End` go to the first and last page.
- `Up` and `Down` scroll a little.

## Zoom and fit

- `Ctrl` + mouse wheel, or `+` and `-`, zooms in and out.
- `Ctrl` + `0` fits the page width, `F` fits the whole page, and `Ctrl` + `1` shows the page at 100%.

## Rotate and pan

`R` rotates the page by 90 degrees. Drag with the left mouse button to pan when the page is larger than the window.

`Left` and `Right` open the previous or next PDF in the folder.
