## Video

MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI files open in the video player. The bar under the picture has a scrubber, a frame counter (click it to switch between frames and timecode), play and pause, and a speed control.

![The video player with its control bar](media:video-player.webp "The frame counter switches between frame numbers and timecode when you click it.")

- **Step frames**: the arrow keys or `,` and `.` step one frame at a time.
- **Loop**: set an in point with `[` or `I` and an out point with `]` or `O`. The clip then loops between them. `\` clears the loop.
- **Safe areas**: turn on the action-safe (90%) and title-safe (80%) guides, and the aspect-ratio guides, for checking framing.
- **Fit**: choose how the video fits the window, and reveal the file in Explorer from the same bar.
- **Next and previous**: move to the next or previous video in the folder.

You can also drag the scrubber in Quick Look, and the video keeps playing while you scrub. See [Quick Look](@deep/quick-look).

## Audio

WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, AAC, AIFF and M4A open in the audio player, with a waveform and the file properties.

![The audio player with its waveform](media:audio-player.webp "The waveform shows the level over the whole file.")

- **Seamless loop** (`L`) repeats the file without a gap, which is useful for checking game loops.
- **Pitch** changes the pitch in semitones without changing the speed. Click the value to reset it to 0.
- Previous and next move through the folder; stop returns to the start.
