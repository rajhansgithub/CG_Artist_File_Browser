## Open a folder or a network share

Click a drive in the sidebar, or press `Ctrl` + `L` and type the path. For a share, type it like File Explorer does, for example `\\mypc\library`. Shares you open are kept in the sidebar. If Windows will not let the app in, use the **+** next to Network locations to sign in. [Folders and network drives](@deep/folders-and-network#network-shares) explains the details.

## Preview a file without leaving the folder

Select it and press `Space`. Arrow keys move on, and `Esc` or `Space` closes the window. [Quick Look](@deep/quick-look) covers video scrubbing and moving the window.

## Open a file in its full viewer

Double-click it, or select it and press `Enter`.

## Look at a 3D model

Open a `.glb`, `.obj`, `.fbx`, `.stl` or `.ply` file. Drag with the left mouse button to orbit, the right button to pan and the wheel to zoom. Press `F` to frame the model. [3D models](@deep/3d-models) lists the shading, light and camera options.

## Open a USD or Alembic file

Install Blender, then check the path in Settings. The app converts `.usd`, `.abc` and `.blend` files to a model in the background and keeps the result. [USD and Alembic](@deep/usd-outliner) explains what to expect.

## Read a Markdown note or README

Open the `.md` file. Pictures and GIFs next to it show in the page, the outline on the right jumps between headings, and `S` shows the plain text. [Markdown documents](@deep/markdown) covers links, network drives and the keys.

## Read an HDR or EXR map

Open it. It appears as a 360° sky. Hold `E` and drag to change the exposure, and use the tone-mapping list in the panel on the right to change how the image is squeezed onto the screen. [HDR and EXR](@deep/hdr-exr) has the rest.

## Compare two versions of a render

Press **A** on one card and **B** on the other, then press **Compare**. Press `1` to `4` to change the mode. [Compare](@deep/compare) covers all of them.

## Add a colour label or a tag

Click the small tag button on a card, pick a colour, or type a tag and press `Enter`. To see only labelled files, use the label filter in the top bar. [Labels and tags](@deep/labels-tags) explains where they are saved.

## Change a folder's icon

Right-click the folder, choose **Change icon…**, and pick one from the library or import your own PNG, SVG, ICO, WebP or JPG. [Folders and network drives](@deep/folders-and-network#folder-icons) has the details.

## Convert a batch of images

Select the images, right-click one of them and choose **Convert…**. Pick the format and the output folder. Existing files are never overwritten. [Batch converter](@deep/batch-converter) lists the formats.

## Send a file to another app

Right-click the file and use **Send to**. The apps come from Settings, where you can add or remove them. [Send to apps](@deep/send-to-apps) explains how to add one.

## Delete or rename a file

Press `F2` to rename. Press `Delete` to move the selection to the Recycle Bin after a confirmation. `Shift` + `Delete` removes it for good. The app never deletes a drive or a share root.

## Change the look

Open Settings (the gear, top right) and choose a theme under Appearance. [Settings and themes](@deep/settings-themes) lists the four options.

## Find a shortcut

Press `?`, or open the [keyboard shortcut card](@quick/shortcuts) and type what you want to do.

## Open this guide from the app

Click the question-mark **Help** button in the top bar. The guide opens in your default browser and works offline. It is also under Documentation in Settings and at the bottom of the shortcut card. [Settings and themes](@deep/settings-themes#documentation) has the details.
