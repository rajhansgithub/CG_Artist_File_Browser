Inspector

The details panel beside the grid: what it shows for each file type, how to open it and how to resize it.

Just the basics?A quick tour →

Opening it

Press I, or click the info button in the top bar. The Inspector opens on the right of the folder. It shows the details of the selected file and updates as you move the selection. Close it with I again or the cross at the top.

What it shows

The fields depend on the type of file:

  • All files: type, size, date modified and the full path.
  • Images and textures: resolution, colour, channels, sample type and compression.
  • EXR: parts and channels, with the file's layers.
  • Video and audio: duration and media information.
  • Folders: the folder's created and modified dates.

Resizing

Drag the left edge of the Inspector to make it wider or narrower.

Tip: The Inspector and the viewers' own side panels are different. The HDR and EXR viewer, for example, has its own panel with the exposure and tone-mapping controls. See HDR and EXR maps.
