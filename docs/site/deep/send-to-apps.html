Send to apps

Opening files in Photoshop, Blender or any other program from the right-click menu, and how to add or remove apps.

Just the basics?How do I…? →

Sending a file

Right-click a file and choose the app under Send to. The file opens in that app.

The apps list

Open Settings and look at External apps. The list starts with the apps the app finds on your PC when it first runs, such as Photoshop or Blender. Removing an app from the list keeps it removed, so it does not come back on the next start.

To add one, press Browse, choose the program's .exe, give it a name, and press Add. The app appears in the Send to menu straight away. Scan looks for installed apps again.
