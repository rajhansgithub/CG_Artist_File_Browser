Quick start

How do I…?

Short answers to the everyday questions, each with the place to look if the answer is not enough.

Want the details?Folders and network drives →

Open a folder or a network share

Click a drive in the sidebar, or press Ctrl + L and type the path. For a share, type it like File Explorer does, for example \\mypc\library. Shares you open are kept in the sidebar. If Windows will not let the app in, use the + next to Network locations to sign in. Folders and network drives explains the details.

Preview a file without leaving the folder

Select it and press Space. Arrow keys move on, and Esc or Space closes the window. Quick Look covers video scrubbing and moving the window.

Open a file in its full viewer

Double-click it, or select it and press Enter.

Look at a 3D model

Open a .glb, .obj, .fbx, .stl or .ply file. Drag with the left mouse button to orbit, the right button to pan and the wheel to zoom. Press F to frame the model. 3D models lists the shading, light and camera options.

Open a USD or Alembic file

Install Blender, then check the path in Settings. The app converts .usd, .abc and .blend files to a model in the background and keeps the result. USD and Alembic explains what to expect.

Read a Markdown note or README

Open the .md file. Pictures and GIFs next to it show in the page, the outline on the right jumps between headings, and S shows the plain text. Markdown documents covers links, network drives and the keys.

Read an HDR or EXR map

Open it. It appears as a 360° sky. Hold E and drag to change the exposure, and use the tone-mapping list in the panel on the right to change how the image is squeezed onto the screen. HDR and EXR has the rest.

Compare two versions of a render

Press A on one card and B on the other, then press Compare. Press 1 to 4 to change the mode. Compare covers all of them.

Add a colour label or a tag

Click the small tag button on a card, pick a colour, or type a tag and press Enter. To see only labelled files, use the label filter in the top bar. Labels and tags explains where they are saved.

Change a folder's icon

Right-click the folder, choose Change icon…, and pick one from the library or import your own PNG, SVG, ICO, WebP or JPG. Folders and network drives has the details.

Convert a batch of images

Select the images, right-click one of them and choose Convert…. Pick the format and the output folder. Existing files are never overwritten. Batch converter lists the formats.

Find duplicate folders and files

Right-click a folder and choose Find duplicates…. Pick Fast (same name and size) or Exact (same contents), then how alike folders must be. The report lists matching folders with a 0 to 100% score and loose duplicate files. Nothing is moved or deleted. Duplicate finder explains the score.

Send a file to another app

Right-click the file and use Send to. The apps come from Settings, where you can add or remove them. Send to apps explains how to add one.

Delete or rename a file

Press F2 to rename. Press Delete to move the selection to the Recycle Bin after a confirmation. Shift + Delete removes it for good. The app never deletes a drive or a share root.

Change the look

Open Settings (the gear, top right) and choose a theme under Appearance. Settings and themes lists the four options.

Find a shortcut

Press ?, or open the keyboard shortcut card and type what you want to do.

Open this guide from the app

Click the question-mark Help button in the top bar. The guide opens in your default browser and works offline. It is also under Documentation in Settings and at the bottom of the shortcut card. Settings and themes has the details.
